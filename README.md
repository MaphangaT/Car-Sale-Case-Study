# Car-Sale-Case-Study

# Car Sales Dashboard – Project Overview

##  Project Description

This project presents a comprehensive analysis of car sales performance using an interactive dashboard.
The goal is to provide clear, data-driven insights into sales trends, product performance, and regional distribution to support executive decision-making.

---

## Objectives

* Monitor overall sales performance (Revenue & Units Sold)
* Identify top-performing car models and brands
* Analyze regional sales distribution
* Understand customer purchasing patterns
* Detect trends, seasonality, and underperformance areas

---

##  Dashboard Overview

The dashboard is designed with an executive focus, providing both high-level KPIs and detailed drill-down insights.

### Key KPI Metrics

* Total Revenue
* Total Units Sold
* Average Selling Price
* Total Orders
* Growth Rate (Month-over-Month)

---

### Sales Trends

* Time-series analysis of revenue and sales volume
* Identification of peak and low-performing periods
* Seasonal patterns in car sales

---

### Product Performance

* Top-selling car models by revenue and quantity
* Brand-level comparison
* Contribution of each model to total sales

---

### Regional Analysis

* Sales distribution by province/region
* Identification of high-performing and low-performing areas
* Market concentration insights

---

### Customer Insights

* Purchase behavior trends
* Average spend per customer
* Customer segmentation (if available)

---

### Pricing Analysis

* Distribution of selling prices
* Relationship between price and demand
* Identification of optimal pricing ranges

---

### Dealer Performance

* Top-performing dealerships
* Revenue contribution by dealer/store
* Performance comparison across locations

---

## Interactivity Features

* Filters: Date, Region, Brand, Model, Dealer
* Drill-down capabilities for deeper analysis
* Dynamic visuals that update based on selections

---

## Key Insights (Example – Replace with your real insights)

* Sales peak during year-end periods, indicating seasonal demand
* Certain models dominate revenue contribution
* Specific regions outperform others consistently
* Mid-range priced vehicles generate the highest volume

---

## Challenges & Limitations

* Missing or incomplete data fields
* Limited customer demographic data
* Potential inconsistencies in pricing or entries

---

## Recommendations

* Increase inventory for high-demand models
* Improve marketing in low-performing regions
* Optimize pricing strategies based on demand patterns
* Focus on customer retention strategies

---

## Tools & Technologies

* Power BI / Tableau / Excel (Dashboarding)
* SQL (Data Extraction & Transformation)
* Data Cleaning Techniques

---

## Conclusion

This dashboard provides a clear, actionable view of car sales performance, enabling stakeholders to make informed strategic decisions and identify growth opportunities.
